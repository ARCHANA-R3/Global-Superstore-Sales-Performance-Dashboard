# 📊 NGO Donation Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
The **NGO Donation Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive **Tableau** dashboard developed to analyze donation trends, revenue generation, beneficiary engagement, and regional performance.

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of how donations are collected, distributed, and utilized, enabling NGOs to improve transparency, optimize resource allocation, and maximize social impact through data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objective
The objective of this dashboard is to:
- Track total donations and revenue performance.
- Analyze donor satisfaction and engagement.
- Evaluate category-wise and sub-category performance.
- Monitor state-wise contribution and impact.
- Identify high-performing regions and programs.
- Support strategic planning for fundraising and outreach.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Tableau
- Microsoft Excel / CSV
- Data Cleaning
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Dashboard Design

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- Interactive filters for:
  - Category
  - Year
  - State / Region

- KPI Cards displaying:
  - Total Volume Sold
  - Average Satisfaction
  - Total Revenue

- Category Trend by Year Analysis
- State-wise Performance Overview (Quantity vs Revenue)
- Sub-Category Profit vs Rating Analysis
- Revenue Analysis by Sub-Category
- Regional Quality & Quantity Heatmap
- State-wise Profit vs Revenue Distribution
- Dynamic cross-filtering across all visualizations

## 📈 Key Insights
### Overall Performance
- **Total Revenue:** ₹4.21M+
- **Total Volume Sold:** 8,185
- **Average Satisfaction:** 3.1

### Category Trends
- Category performance varies significantly between **2023–2026**.
- Some categories demonstrate consistent growth, while others fluctuate across the years.

### State-wise Performance
- States such as **Illinois** and **Idaho** contribute higher donation volumes.
- Revenue distribution varies independently from donation quantity.
- Some states generate higher revenue despite comparatively lower donation volumes.

### Sub-Category Performance
- **Type1** and **Type2** generate the highest profits (approximately ₹600K+).
- **Type3** delivers comparatively lower profit while maintaining moderate satisfaction ratings.
- Higher profit does not always correspond to higher donor satisfaction.

### Revenue Analysis
- **Type2** contributes the largest share of total revenue (approximately ₹1.43M).
- Revenue is concentrated among a few high-performing sub-categories.

### Regional Insights
- The **South** region records higher average satisfaction ratings.
- The **North** region performs strongly in **Type3**.
- Overall regional performance remains balanced with slight variations.

### Distribution Analysis
- Most states fall within the mid-range for both profit and revenue.
- States such as **Vermont** and **Wisconsin** stand out as high-performing outliers.
- These insights highlight opportunities for targeted fundraising and regional expansion.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Improve donor satisfaction through personalized engagement and communication strategies.
- Focus fundraising campaigns on high-performing states to maximize donations.
- Expand outreach initiatives in underperforming regions.
- Invest additional resources in high-revenue sub-categories such as **Type2**.
- Optimize lower-performing categories using targeted awareness campaigns.
- Utilize regional insights to improve resource allocation and program planning.
